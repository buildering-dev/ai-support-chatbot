# Showroom and servicing

## Showroom address and opening hours
Our showroom and workshop is at 12 Example Lane, Marrickville NSW (a fictional address for this demo).
Monday to Friday: 9am-6pm.
Saturday: 9am-4pm.
Sunday and public holidays: closed.

## Servicing
A first service is free within 3 months of purchase. After that, a standard service costs $119 and an e-bike service (including a battery and motor health check) costs $149.

Book a service online or call the workshop. Most services are done the same day if you drop the bike off before 10am.

## Payment options
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal and Afterpay. Afterpay is available on orders up to $2,000.

## Contact
Email hello@harbourbikes.example. We reply within 1 business day.
