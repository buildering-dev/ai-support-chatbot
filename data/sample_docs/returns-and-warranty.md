# Returns and warranty

## 30-day returns
You can return any bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if it has been ridden less than 50 km and is in as-new condition. Return shipping is free from Sydney metro; interstate returns cost $79.

Ridden bikes over 50 km can still be returned within 30 days, but a 15% restocking fee applies.

Accessories can be returned unused, in original packaging, within 30 days.

## How to start a return
Email returns@harbourbikes.example with your order number and the reason. We will send a prepaid label (Sydney) or a return quote (interstate) within 1 business day. Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 business days of the bike arriving back.

## Warranty
Frames: 5-year warranty.
Motors and batteries on e-bikes: 2-year warranty.
Other components: 1-year warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover wear and tear (tyres, brake pads, chains), crash damage or damage from incorrect servicing.

## Making a warranty claim
Email warranty@harbourbikes.example with your order number, photos of the problem and a short description. Most claims are answered within 2 business days.
