# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Sydney metro orders are delivered free within 2-4 business days. Your bike arrives fully assembled and safety-checked.

Interstate delivery (Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide, Perth, Hobart, Canberra, Darwin) takes 5-8 business days. Bikes ship 90% assembled in a box; you only need to attach the handlebars and pedals. A free online assembly video is included.

Regional and remote areas can take up to 12 business days.

## Delivery costs
Sydney metro: free.
Interstate capital cities: $79 per bike.
Regional and remote areas: $129 per bike.
Accessories only (no bike): free on orders over $50, otherwise $9.95.

## Tracking your order
You will get a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships. Most orders ship within 1 business day of payment.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address until the order ships. Email orders@harbourbikes.example with your order number and the new address.

## International shipping
We currently ship within Australia only.
